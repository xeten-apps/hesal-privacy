Common questions

Quick answers to the things people ask most.

How do I get an account?

Accounts are created by your company’s ERP administrator. The app cannot create accounts.

I forgot my password.

Contact your ERP administrator to reset it.

A module is missing from my dashboard.

The dashboard only shows modules your account has access to. Ask your administrator to grant access, then sign out and sign in again.

I’m asked to sign in again.

For security, sessions expire after a period of time. Sign in again to continue.

How do I remove my data from this phone?

Open Profile and tap Sign out, or uninstall the app.
