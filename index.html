Hesal is a mobile companion app for the ERP system of HESAL CONTRACTING L.L.C (“HESAL”). It is developed and published by Xeten Information Services Private Limited (“Xeten”, “we”, “us”). The app is intended for HESAL staff and authorised users who already have an account on HESAL’s ERP.

1Summary

Only what is needed The app only uses the information needed to sign you in and show the ERP modules you are allowed to use.
No selling, ads or tracking We do not sell your data, show advertising, or use third-party analytics or tracking.
Data stays in the ERP Your business data stays in HESAL’s ERP system; the app is a secure window to it.

2Information the app handles

InformationWhy it is usedWhere it is kept
Username/email and password you enter at sign-in To sign you in to HESAL’s ERP Sent securely (HTTPS) to HESAL’s ERP server. Your password is not stored by the app.
Sign-in token and ERP session identifier To keep you signed in and open ERP screens without signing in again Stored in your device’s encrypted secure storage (iOS Keychain / Android Keystore) until you sign out or it expires.
Profile details returned by the ERP: name, username, email, company, user ID and module permissions To show your name and the modules you have access to Stored on your device until you sign out.
Remembered username (optional) To pre-fill the sign-in screen if you choose “Remember my username” Stored on your device until you turn it off.
ERP records you view, create or edit (e.g. sales orders, projects, payslips), and any files you choose to attach To let you use the ERP from your phone Kept in HESAL’s ERP system, not by Xeten. The app does not keep separate copies.

The app does not access your location, contacts, microphone, or other personal data on your device. If you choose to attach a photo or file to an ERP record, the app only accesses the item you select, and only to upload it to the ERP.

3How information is shared

Information is only sent between your device and HESAL’s ERP server at erp.hesaluae.com. We do not share it with advertisers, data brokers, or analytics providers. Information may be disclosed if required by law.

Your use of the ERP itself, and how HESAL handles the business and employee data inside it, is governed by HESAL’s own internal policies and your employment or service agreement.

4Security

All communication uses encrypted HTTPS connections. Sign-in credentials are stored in the operating system’s secure storage. Your session ends automatically when it expires, and signing out removes the stored sign-in data from your device.

5Data retention and deletion

On your device

Signing out or uninstalling the app deletes the data the app stored.

In the ERP

User accounts are created and managed by HESAL. The app cannot create accounts. To close your account or request deletion of your data, contact HESAL’s administrator or us using the details below.

6Children

The app is intended for business use by employees and authorised users, and is not directed at children.

7Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
